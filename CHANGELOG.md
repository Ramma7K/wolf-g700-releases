# Changelog

## 3.08 (211) — 31 October 2026

## What's new
- Optional steering-wheel media controls: play/pause, next/previous track, and hold to seek when supported by the player.
- Choose Miles and mph in Appearance for our home speed display, speed widgets and custom gauge. The factory gauge keeps its own units.
- Updated widgets, weather, home-layout tools, backgrounds and quick-tray customization since the previous public release.

## Preview tools
- Try three original engine sounds in Developer → Audio & parked steering lab → Engine audio preview. Choose a short demo or live engine RPM; leaving the screen stops playback.
- A timed OEM-selector marker is available in the developer theme probe for parked display testing. It does not replace the factory dashboard or resolve native warning-layer placement.

## Before using
- Steering media controls are off by default and require notification access and a compatible active player. Test while parked; disable them if the factory also handles the same press.
- Engine audio is an experimental foreground preview using the current media output. In-car sound quality and latency are not yet verified; the emulator playback check could not establish playback while its audio service was failing.
- Physical steering compatibility and OEM-selector behavior still require parked testing on the car.

## 2.80 (183)

## Before updating
- The quick tray starts with the new default layout, including BeepBox beside Cluster on/off. Customize it again in Tray buttons or use Restore default tray layout.
- Open the app after updating to release any old removal protection.

## Easier uninstall
- Protect from removal has been removed.
- Remove opens Android uninstall without requiring system access or choosing another home app first.
- Car fallback is available if the car does not show an uninstall screen.

## Better
- Home-screen arrangements are saved when you press Done.
- BeepBox has complete replacement recordings, custom MP3/WAV support and an evenly spaced sound grid.
- All media output offers connected Bluetooth and other audio devices on supported systems. Availability depends on the car and playback app.
- The quick tray has consistent spacing and a default BeepBox shortcut.

## Wolf G700
- A floating circular shortcut menu opens from the Wolf icon.
- Arrange the connected Wolf and Back buttons vertically or horizontally.
- The Wolf logo appears in the home-screen header.
